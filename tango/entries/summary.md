# TWIST THE TANGO — Summary

**Logline.** Friday, 5PM, Toronto. A junior analyst stuck late at the office chasing a takeover rumour and his best friend half-painted blue at the Jays game spend one night twisting between the trade of a lifetime and everything softer they keep not choosing.

**Form.** Episodic series. Created and written by Cairns Nolan. A Blue Sheep Films development title. Episode 1 — "4th House on Grand St" — script in progress (through Scene 7 of an 18-beat outline).

---

## The shape

Two threads, one night, intercut.

**Vincent (25)** is the last body in an emptied office — hair losing its shape, eyes as red as his tie, waiting on a call from UNKNOWN. When it comes, it's the rumour: SayFlair's takeover has allegedly gone through, sourced from someone's aunt, and it isn't actionable until Monday. Which gives Vincent all weekend to do the thing he shouldn't: confirm it.

**Corey (26)** is outside the stadium in face paint meeting a girl off an app who's already fact-checking his height. Hotdogs at urinals, a joint before the gates, pink lipgloss. His phone stays quiet — until Vincent's text lands: *News on Sayflair?*

The episode's centerpiece is the call between them — split screen, diagonal — the trading-floor thread and the bleachers thread finally in one frame. Act II sends Vincent home past an ice-cream meet-cute (Renee) and puts Corey back at the office with his assistant. Act III comes home to Dani, who rips Corey and doesn't put up with any of it, and ends on a small moment in front of the TV — the pink lipgloss again.
