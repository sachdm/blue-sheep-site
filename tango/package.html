WHAT IT IS

A series pilot. One Friday evening in Toronto, 5:00PM to 7:45PM, cut between two 25-year-olds: Vincent, alone in an emptied office, gambling his career on a rumour — and Corey, at a baseball game on a date he's barely present for. One is trying to become someone. The other is already comfortable. They are the same age and the same amount of alone.
13 pp ≈ 13 min · 4-day board · Toronto · a pilot, not a short — which changes what it needs and who funds it.
[!] TWO SCRIPT QUESTIONS BEFORE THIS IS COSTED AS FINAL. The PDF stops at a scene 9 marker with nothing under it — either the pilot ends on Vincent walking out (a strong ending) or pages are missing. And Corey's thread currently runs parallel without converging: he's a life, not yet a plot. Both are answerable in an afternoon; neither should be answered by a budget.

Where the money is

Locations (amber) are unusually high for a 13-page script — that's the stadium. Everything else is a standard Toronto interior build. If the ballpark becomes a rented venue or a minor-league park, roughly $2,000 comes straight off the top.

DayBoard
