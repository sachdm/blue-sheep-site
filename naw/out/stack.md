# NOT ANOTHER WORD — production stack

**Short film · 12 pp · Toronto · non-union / ACTRA TIP · CAD**
Sized for the v4 build (5½ days + prep day, four locations). Mostly free or one-time tools; the money stays on sound and the performance days.

## Secured

These four lines are carried in the budget at full value and netted out of the cash need. See `budget.html`.

## Things this script needs that a generic short does not

- **Talk-show playback.** Shoot the talk-show insert on the prep day, not on the D6 pickup day, so it plays live on the pub TV from a laptop over HDMI on D5.
- **Radio news and voicemail.** Record in post with the mixer. Scratch audio on set for timing only.
- **The obituary.** Real newsprint sells the beat. Lay it out in Affinity and print through a short-run newsprint service. Photograph the actor for the Lombok shot and composite the boat behind him. Krea can build the background; the face has to be the real actor.
- **The van sign.** A magnetic DRAIN DISPOSAL panel and the permanent painted logo underneath are two vinyl orders from one shop.
- **Across-the-street windows.** Either a second small unit with two extras and a permission, or cut to the van alone. Decide before the board.
