# NOT ANOTHER WORD — Production Package (working)

**Working document, 1 Aug 2026. Priced against short film v1.3. All figures CAD, Toronto-based, pre-quote estimates — this is a planning tool, not a locked budget.**

---

## The shape of the shoot

Estimated runtime 15–16 minutes from 17 pages. One hero location carries roughly seventy percent of the film: a period-dressed apartment with a working kitchen, study, bedroom, and a patio or balcony with a street view. The rest: one pub night, one street run for the vespa, and one dawn exterior for the van.

**Schedule: 5 shoot days.**

- Days 1–3 — Apartment (routine, McGarr call, study, the interview, the reset room). The interview alone is a full day.
- Day 4 — Asters Pub (night). Extras call, TV playback, child actor.
- Day 5 — Splits: vespa street run (magic hour), van/gurney exterior (dawn), pickups.

## Cast (9 speaking + VO)

Miles (lead, all 5 days), Stacey, Michael x2, Joansy, Shari, Child, plus VO: McGarr, Host, Newsman, Radio. Background: ~15 pub extras, the couple and family in the windows (half-day second unit or shot from the patio).

The film is Miles. Budget assumes one meaningful cast expense: a recognizable lead who can hold the interview. Everything else can be strong local theatre casting.

## Production challenges (the honest list)

- **Smoking on camera** — constant. Herbal cigarettes, ventilation, and it must be cleared with the location and insurer up front.
- **Child actor** — Ontario rules: limited hours, tutor/parent on set. Schedule the pub scene around the kid, not the reverse.
- **Vespa run** — moving vehicle shot at night. Process trailer is the safe version; locked-off + lens tricks is the cheap version. Budgeted cheap, flagged risky.
- **The van + gurney at dawn** — permits for the street, one art-department hero prop (magnetic DRAIN DISPOSAL sign over painted SIDMAN'S logo — the sign IS the ending; build two).
- **Period texture** — rotary phone, radio, crystal, tan suits, the Yunnan Dragon (custom prop build). No cars-in-frame problem if framed tight.

- Crew (skeleton 8, day rates) — $6,000
- Art / props / wardrobe (Dragon build, van signs, period dressing) — $3,000
- Locations (apartment favour, pub buyout one night) — $2,000
- Camera / lighting / grip package — $2,500
- Sound (mixer + kit) — $1,200
- Catering / craft — $1,500

- Post (edit favour, colour + mix flat deals, music) — $2,500
- Deliverables + festival entry round — $1,000
- Contingency (~10%) — $2,000 → **total lands ~$26,500 with contingency**

### Tier 2 — Standard indie (paid crew, ACTRA TIP possible): $58,000  ← recommended

- Cast (TIP rates, name-ish lead 5 days) — $7,500
- Crew (14 heads × 5 days, standard indie rates) — $16,000
- Art / props / wardrobe / picture vehicle (vespa rental) — $6,500
- Locations (apartment 4 days, pub buyout, street permits) — $6,000
- Camera / lighting / grip (proper package + process trailer half-day) — $6,500
- Sound — $2,500
- Transport / vans — $1,500
- Catering / craft (full crew, 5 days) — $3,500
- Insurance + permits + payroll — $3,000
- Post (editor 3 wks, colour, full mix, composer) — $8,500
- Deliverables (DCP, captions, stills) + festival strategy — $2,000
- Contingency (10%) — $5,800

### Tier 3 — Polished / union: $95,000–$110,000

Full ACTRA, IATSE-adjacent rates, name lead with an offer that costs something, 6 days instead of 5, real process trailer night, post house colour and mix, festival publicist. The film doesn't change; the margin for error and the doors it opens do.

## Financing avenues

Canada Council and Ontario Arts Council media-arts grants fit this profile (single location, director-driven, producible proof-of-concept for the pilot). Shorts don't meaningfully access tax credits — don't build the plan around them. The honest pitch: this short is the proof-of-concept for the NOT ANOTHER WORD pilot, and the budget should be framed to investors as slate development, not standalone recoupment.

## Rights flag (repeat of Log 01)

"Based on the life of Hunter S. Thompson" needs a counsel conversation before a public shoot or festival submission. Budget line for legal review lives in insurance/permits at Tier 2+.
