THE FINDING THAT CHANGES THE SLATE

TAC's rule: "The only eligible applicant for a film or video project is the director. Producers may not apply as co-applicants." You produce Together, Alone — you cannot be the applicant. John Cardillo Jr must apply.
That is good news, not bad. John's council slots are separate from yours, so Together, Alone at TAC does not consume the slot NOT ANOTHER WORD needs. Both films can go to TAC in the same cycle under different applicants. Confirm this with Peter Kingstone before you rely on it.
And a conflict to avoid: the completion sheet is tracking CAVCO. OAC bars projects seeking tax credits outright. So Together, Alone goes TAC + Canada Council + tax credits, and stays away from OAC entirely — which conveniently leaves NOMAD's Oct 8 OAC slot untouched.
