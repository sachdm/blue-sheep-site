We make films the slow way.

Who we are

Blue Sheep is a production company in Toronto, founded in 2023. A small crew who believe the best stories are found, not forced — shot on real locations, with real people, at the pace the story actually needs.

We have kept the team small on purpose. Every project gets the same attention, whether it is a ninety-second spot or a feature doc, and everything is cut in the same room by the people who shot it.

What we do

Development
Scripts, coverage, beat sheets, financing plans and grant packages.
Production
Small units on real locations. Narrative shorts, commercial and music film.
Post
Edit, colour, sound and delivery — through to festival and distribution.
