## Casting note (this is the whole film)

Chemistry reads before anything else. Two actors who can play eight months of history in how they hand each other a coffee. The couch-wedge beat and the hug are the two auditions scenes.

## Assumptions & exclusions

- The couch carry is real — stairwell location must allow it; stunt-free but rehearsed.
- Radio music diegetic and low: budget assumes library or one indie license, not a needle-drop.
- Excludes reshoots, publicist, EPK. No tax-credit assumption.
