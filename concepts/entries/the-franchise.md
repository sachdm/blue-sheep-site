# THE FRANCHISE — CONCEPT ONE-PAGER

**series & features compartment · Blue Sheep Films**

---

**Format:** half-hour series · **Compartment:** concepts

**Logline:** Dad's empire — three shawarma shops and a parking lot — needs a successor while he recovers from heart surgery and refuses to name one. Two sons and a cousin with "ideas" go to war over garlic sauce, and the walk-in fridge becomes the boardroom.

**Engine:** every Succession move has a strip-mall equivalent. The hostile takeover is a lunch rush poached from the flagship location. The board coup is the aunties. The proxy fight is whose photo goes on the new menus. Dad watches it all from a recliner, delighted, healthier by the week, in no hurry — the Logan move: the succession crisis IS his cardio.

**The three:** the eldest, who knows the sauce recipe and thinks that's the crown; the younger one, who actually runs the numbers and is invisible for it; and the cousin — ghost kitchens, an app, a vest — who has never closed a till in his life and might win anyway, because he's the only one dad finds interesting.

**Casting:** two brothers and a cousin, 23-28, from a community whose family businesses actually look like this — cast authentically and the show writes itself.

**Feasibility:** one real shawarma shop shot overnight buys the whole world. The pilot's cold open — the heart attack during Friday rush — is a fundable short on its own.
