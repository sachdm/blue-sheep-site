## Safety notes (read first)

- Certified lifeguard-of-record on deck every wet minute; water-safety officer separate from cast supervision.
- Kai's "struggle" beat choreographed standing-depth with camera trickery; the actor can swim even though the character can't.
- Night water temperature: heated pool or wetsuit-under scheduling; a cold 12-year-old ends the night, and should.

## Assumptions & exclusions

- Excludes reshoots, publicist, EPK. No tax-credit assumption.
