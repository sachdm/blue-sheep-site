THE SHORT VERSION

Two films, two councils, two deadlines nine and ten weeks out. NAW → TAC, Oct 15, ask $15,000. NOMAD → OAC, Oct 8, ask $10,000. Both also go to Canada Council. One decision — the tax-credit question — determines whether that split holds. Everything else is assembly.
Nothing here costs money before a letter arrives. The only spend in the next ten weeks is optional: one hour of a production accountant's time in mid-September.
