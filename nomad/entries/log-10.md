The notes said the script was falling apart, and they were right about where: the handshake was cute but shallow, Ted's midnight standup broke physics, the CDMX agent contradicted Michelle handling everything, a jokey bracket call landed AFTER Will saw Cairns wrecked on a curb, and the paradise arrived with no winter to earn it. v3.5 fixes the machine:

- **The device, replaced.** The handshake is gone. The new ritual is the verification game — "Prove it's you," answered with things only they'd know — invented the night of the golf video, taught through John, used at the elevator, at the curb, and turned inside out twice: the likeness passes the test PERFECTLY (the real Cairns always told the story wrong — perfection is the tell), and street-Cairns begs the game at strangers who don't know the rules. John's last line to the dead floor is the game with nobody left to verify. Deeper, and it IS the theme: verification is Will's job, his ritual, and finally his wound.
- **Commercial appeal first.** Cold open is the rooftop-pool paradise — then a smash to a horizontal GTA blizzard eating Will alive. The blizzard recurs: the Service storefront (same storm), Cairns' one-shot Beck-taxi exit, and Will's departure mirroring it shot for shot.
- **Ted, legalized.** The 1 AM standup is Singapore's mid-morning now, said by the clock in the corner of the call. The beat plants the premise instead of breaking the clock.
- **CDMX, no agent.** Michelle handles everything; the door unlocks from his phone. The height marks survive as one strip of pencil the painters missed inside a closet — displacement as evidence, not as a leasing agent's small talk.

Mixer → bracket call (same party, so the joke tracks) → street sighting → instant warm text from "Oaxaca" while the man is on the curb → likeness call → investigation. The stakes now climb a staircase instead of teleporting.
- **Show, don't verify.** The street scene's app exposition is cut; the audience gets the game, not a readout to pick apart.
