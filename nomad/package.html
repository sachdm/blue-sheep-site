Where the money is

Crew is 27% of the build and non-negotiable at 10 days. Screens/UI (amber) is 11% and protected — it is the film. The three smallest lines are where scrappy savings live, and they total less than the UI budget.

Cash-flow curve

The curve is front-loaded by design: OAC releases nothing until all revenue is confirmed, so the private gap must close BEFORE the pre-pro spend starts. Contingency is held to the end and reported as surplus if unspent (OAC claws back surpluses over $250).
