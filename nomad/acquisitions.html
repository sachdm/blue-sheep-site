SHORT STORY ACQUISITIONS

building an IP library on Canadian short fiction · dev pipeline · action plan

WHY SHORT STORIES

A short story option runs $500–$2,500 for 12–18 months. A literary novel starts around $5,000 and climbs. A story is also the right shape — it fits the shorts pipeline, and "adapted from" reads well to OAC, TAC and Telefilm because it proves you can work with material from outside your own head.
Total exposure to build a three-title library: roughly $4,500 in options plus one legal fee. Cheaper than a single shoot day.

The standard offer — hold it, don't negotiate against yourself

TermBlue Sheep standardWhy
Option fee$1,500Real money to a small-press author; survivable if the film never happens.
Option period18 monthsCovers one full grant cycle plus a shoot window.
Extension$750 / 12 monthsOne extension only. Open-ended options make authors hostile.
Purchase price$7,500Payable on financing close, not on signature. Costs nothing until there's money.
RightsShort film, worldwide, all mediaDo not ask for feature or series rights on a short. It doubles the price and the anxiety.
ReversionAutomaticIf unmade at expiry, rights revert clean. Say this in the first email — it is why people say yes.
Credit"Based on the story by ___"Author approves the wording.

The approach

approach email — copy this
Subject: Film option enquiry — "[STORY TITLE]"

Dear [NAME],

I'm a Toronto filmmaker and I run Blue Sheep Films. I've just finished reading [COLLECTION] and I'd like to ask about film rights to one story specifically: "[STORY TITLE]."

[Two sentences on why that story and what the film would be. Concrete, not flattering. Name the image or the moment you can't stop thinking about.]

What I'm proposing is a short film — roughly 12 to 15 minutes, shot in Ontario, financed through the Toronto Arts Council and Canada Council, festival-first. Our current slate is at sachdm.github.io/blue-sheep-site if you'd like to see the kind of work we make.

My offer would be $1,500 for an 18-month option, with a purchase price of $7,500 payable if the film is financed, and a "based on the story by [NAME]" credit. You'd have approval on the credit and I'd send you the script before we shoot — not for approval, just because you should see it.

If the rights aren't available or this isn't of interest, no need to reply at length — a one-line no is genuinely fine and I'll leave you alone.

Best,
Sacha Mitchell
Blue Sheep Films · sacha@bluesheepfilms.com
Name one story, not the collection — it signals you actually read it and you're not fishing. State the reversion up front. Give them an easy no; it gets you more yeses than a hard sell does.
