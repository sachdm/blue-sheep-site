The hundred passes came down to protecting one idea: the form is the structure. Every section header on that application is a beat, and every beat had to earn its scene — passes 1-30 cut anything the paperwork didn't force into the open. Frank never lectures; the Canadian Tire story arrives as fact, not moral, because the panels kept flagging any draft where the father explains the theme. "Miss one. See what happens." held against thirty alternate closers. The ending was originally the signature itself — flat; the photocopy pinned where the drawings went is the emotion doing paperwork, which is the only way this family does emotion. Final trim target: the clock tick should be the loudest line in the scene, and by pass 100 it is.
