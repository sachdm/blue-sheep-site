# THE COTTAGE — CONCEPT ONE-PAGER

**series & features compartment · Blue Sheep Films**

---

**Format:** half-hour series (or a contained feature) · **Compartment:** concepts — separate pool from the shorts slate

**Logline:** A grandfather's will leaves the family cottage to "whichever of you can keep it." Four grandsons — broke, urban, and allergic to each other — get one summer to prove maintenance, and the boathouse becomes the boardroom.

**Engine:** Succession dynamics at a scale a mid-20s cast can plausibly fight over. The stakes are a dock, a roof, and a legacy — everything to them, almost nothing in dollars, which is where the comedy lives. Every episode is a repair that becomes a negotiation: the septic bed is a hostile takeover, the beer run is a summit, the Labour Day closing is the season finale by definition.

**The four:** the one who summered there every year and believes that's equity; the one with money (relative — he has a line of credit) who believes that's equity; the one grandpa actually liked; and the cousin nobody remembers being invited. Alliances rotate weekly. The will's executor — a small-town lawyer who fishes — is the season's Gerri.

**Casting:** four men, 24-28, ensemble-first. The show is auditionable with two lawn chairs and a dock.

**Feasibility:** one hero location that IS the production value. Shoot a proof-of-concept short at any borrowed cottage in September when rentals go quiet.
