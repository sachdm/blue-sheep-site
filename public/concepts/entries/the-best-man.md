# THE BEST MAN — CONCEPT ONE-PAGER

**series & features compartment · Blue Sheep Films**

---

**Format:** short film first (this one is shootable NOW), expandable to a feature · **Compartment:** concepts

**Logline:** A groom must choose one best man from three lifelong friends, and makes the mistake of saying so out loud. Campaigning begins immediately. Full Succession politics, zero infrastructure.

**Engine:** the throne is symbolic, which makes the war absolute. Opposition research (a 2019 screenshot, held in reserve like a nuclear deterrent). A leaked shortlist. A kingmaker fiancée who claims neutrality and runs everything. Debates disguised as poker nights. The dark-horse fourth friend who "doesn't care about that stuff" — polling quietly ahead precisely because of it.

**The three (plus one):** the childhood friend running on seniority; the college friend running on competence (he has a spreadsheet for the bachelor party — a beautiful, terrifying spreadsheet); the work friend running on money; and the dark horse, running on nothing, which turns out to be a platform.

**Casting:** four men, 24-28, one apartment, one bar patio. The audition scene is the poker night.

**Feasibility:** EIGHTY BUCKS money. Two locations, five actors, a weekend. Of the six concepts in this compartment, this is the one that can shoot this month.
