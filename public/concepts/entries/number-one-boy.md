# NUMBER ONE BOY — CONCEPT ONE-PAGER

**series & features compartment · Blue Sheep Films**

---

**Format:** half-hour series · **Compartment:** concepts

**Logline:** A slumlord dies and his three sons inherit one crumbling six-plex — and its tenants, who are organized, ancient, ruthless, and have been running the building for years. The brothers think they inherited an empire. They inherited a senate.

**Engine:** the succession war is fought through work orders. Whoever the tenants obey is the real heir, and the tenants know it — they play the brothers against each other with the patience of people on rent control who will outlive everyone. "Dad ran this building for forty years." / "Dad ran this building into the ground, Danny. We inherited the ground."

**The three:** the eldest, who worked "with" dad for a decade (he held the flashlight); the middle one with a real job who wants to sell and can't say so out loud; the youngest, who the tenants adopt as their candidate precisely because he's the weakest. Kingmaker: the retired schoolteacher in 2B who has minutes from every tenant meeting since 1998.

**Casting:** three men, 24-29, plus a murderer's row of character actors over 60 — the cheapest great casting pool in the country.

**Feasibility:** one building. Hallways, a boiler room, one unit per episode. Pilot provable as a short: the funeral, the reading, and the first clogged drain.
