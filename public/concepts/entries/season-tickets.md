# SEASON TICKETS — CONCEPT ONE-PAGER

**series & features compartment · Blue Sheep Films**

---

**Format:** short film or half-hour pilot · **Compartment:** concepts

**Logline:** Dad's will is precise about the house, the car, and the savings — and completely silent on the two Leafs season tickets held in the family for thirty-one years. Three brothers. Forty-one home games. One pair of seats.

**Engine:** the tickets are the only thing in the estate that can't be split, sold, or valued, which makes them the only thing worth fighting about. The negotiation table is the funeral home, then the garage, then centre ice of a driveway ball-hockey game that becomes a summit. Game-by-game drafts are proposed and collapse. A spreadsheet appears. A spreadsheet is repudiated. The darkest fights aren't about hockey — they're about who took a client to Game 7 instead of Uncle Pete, and everybody remembers.

**The three:** the eldest, who went with dad the most and assumed; the middle one, who paid for dad's parking for a decade and kept receipts (he literally kept the receipts); the youngest, who moved away — and whose claim is that dad always said the seats would keep him coming home. Which is either sentiment or the strongest legal argument in the room.

**Casting:** three brothers, 24-30. One garage, one driveway, one funeral suit each.

**Feasibility:** never needs the arena — the seats themselves stay off-screen, which is the point. Shootable as a short for under $10k.
