# CAP TABLE — CONCEPT ONE-PAGER

**series & features compartment · Blue Sheep Films**

---

**Format:** limited series (6 × 30) or feature · **Compartment:** concepts

**Logline:** Four friends built an app in a two-bedroom on an equity split decided years ago on a napkin nobody kept. Then a real acquisition offer lands — seven figures — and the napkin becomes the Magna Carta.

**Engine:** the offer doesn't change the company, it changes the past. Suddenly the history is contested: who did the work vs. who had the idea vs. who quit for eight months ("sabbatical") vs. whose mom lent the four grand that kept the servers on — and does the mom have a claim? (The mom thinks so. The mom gets a lawyer. The mom's lawyer is the season's best character.)

**The four:** the coder who built everything and negotiated nothing; the "idea guy" whose name is on the incorporation because he showed up that day; the operator who joined late but made it real; and the ghost — the fourth founder who left, signed nothing, and has just seen the news.

**Casting:** four leads, 24-29. The two-bedroom is the Waystar boardroom, and the whiteboard wall is the show's throne room.

**Feasibility:** 90% one apartment. Flashbacks use the same apartment, emptier. The pilot is provable as a short: the offer email arrives during a house meeting about whose turn it is to buy toilet paper.
