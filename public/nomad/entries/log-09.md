**Rendered from the annotated hard copy, 1 Aug 2026.** Thirty-two pages of margin notes, taken with the prescribed grain of salt. What follows is what survived, what didn't, and why.

## The fights we had

**The handshake stays.** The margins wanted it dead ("so f***ing lame," "I hate the fist bump," "replace entirely"). It is not dying. It is the spine: it's how Will catches the likeness, and it's the entire final image. The fix for "feels young" is staging, not deletion — it's now invented later, drunker, more embarrassed. They know it's dumb. That's why it's theirs.

**The golf-night footage stays — with a better plant.** The margins asked the right question: why would they be recording themselves? Answer, v3.4: *Cairns* films it. Handheld, narrating his own course record, then sets the phone down against the stapler mid-scene and forgets it. The only authentic footage of Cairns was shot by Cairns, performing.

**No visible malfunctions.** The treadmill gag is parked. The fakes stay 0.4 off, never broken — the moment one visibly glitches, Will's year of looking away stops being plausible.

**No "beautiful poverty" taxi tour.** The subtitled locals carry it.

- Michelle enters dialogue properly, with three-month probationary terms.

- The mixer is full of people just like him. Everyone got here through a Michelle. Nobody thinks it's the same Michelle.
- The lobby flags Will the moment he walks in. His four minutes were never his.

- "Reserved." is gone. The putter leaning on hole 7 says it.
