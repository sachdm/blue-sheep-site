# NOMAD — Log 14 · The Critique Pass (the second half of v4)

**an honest read of v4, and the seven fixes**

---

## The read: the structure pass alone was 8.6, not 9.1

The structure pass fixed causality and introduced two new problems while leaving three old ones standing. Named honestly:

**1. The 3 AM scene told the audience its own function.** "You'd tell me. If it got weird down there. You'd be the one who could tell." That is not a man talking at 3 AM — that is a screenwriter putting a flag in the ground. The scene was strong until the moment it explained itself.

**2. Will had no want.** The fatal one. Cairns wants out. John wants everything to stay. Lucía wants nothing from anybody. Will drifted, and a protagonist without an active want makes a passive film no matter how clean the causality is.

**3. Will's climax was compliance; the film's climax belonged to John.** Will gets led through a door. John gets the last active choice. That is a point-of-view violation — the audience spent 28 minutes inside Will and then watched someone else finish.

**4. The good days were the weakest writing in the script.** Haggle, overtip, mispronounce. Three clichés of the oblivious tourist. A programmer would write "seen it" in the margin.

**5. The render farm was over-inventoried.** Nine screens described in sequence — the writer showing the world-build instead of the character finding it.

**6. Lucía delivered the thesis with her mouth.** "Somewhere that isn't for sale" is the film's idea, spoken by the film's most alive character, which makes her a device.

**7. Nothing in the film earned the title.**

## The seven fixes

**The ask goes oblique.** Cairns no longer flags the theme. He asks for something small and embarrassing: *"Do the thing sometimes. Down there. Even on a call."* Will tells him he won't need a protocol on a pool deck. "Yeah. No. Obviously." The audience won't know that was the ask until Act Four — which is what an ask at 3 AM actually sounds like.

**Will's want is planted in one gesture.** At the desk, after he overrides the machine and it concedes: *"he smiles. Nobody in the world knows he was right except the machine that just admitted it, and that is enough. It has been enough for nine years."* His want is **to be the one who can tell** — which is precisely why he cannot afford to look at Cairns. The film's engine and its blindness are now the same appetite.

**THE NEW CLIMAX — Will uses the game, and no one in the room knows the story.** In the verification chair, tag coming toward his collar, he finds the only thing he has: *"Ask me something only I'd know. I can prove it."* And he tells Wonderland — true, complete, unarguable. The attendant, not unkindly: **"I don't know who that is."** The only man alive who could verify Will has a version number on a locker eleven feet behind him. Will's arc now ends on his own action and his own failure, and John's *"prove it's you"* to a dead floor lands where it belongs — as a coda, not a hijacking.

**The market is one gesture, not three.** He wins forty pesos off a vendor, then overtips by triple two stalls later, "and does not notice these are the same delight." The Spanish correction stays, sharpened: *"She says it back to him, correctly. He hears a compliment."*

**Render farm cut from nine screens to four**, and his own laugh now arrives through the ear, not the eye — he hears it from the next row before he finds the screen.

**Lucía stops carrying the thesis and gets a brother instead.** Berlin, six years, home every Christmas to talk about the bakeries. *"He's very happy. He's never once stayed till Sunday."* Same idea, personal cost, no thesis.

**The title earns itself once**, in Cairns' mouth, as a joke: *"You know what they call it? The nomad package. Like I'm a guy with a camel."*

## Where it lands: 9.1

Nine-point-one is what a script can be worth. The remaining tenths aren't writing problems — they're the curb performance, the water that doesn't move like water, and 0.4 of a second in a mix. Three things stay deliberately unwritten pending a read: the arrival beat, the slack-off work scene, and Perfect Days texture in the good days. Adding them now would be padding a film that has just stopped needing it.
