## Scrappy tier (the real "us" number)

Crew of friends, deferred everything, owned camera, one paid line: sound. **~$5,500–6,500 all-in.** This film loses nothing at that number if the two performances are right.

## Schedule (one-line strips)

- Day 1 — kitchen + couch scenes (morning texture, game night, the 3 AM hallway) — 3.2 pp
- Day 2 — the confrontation + the notepad scene (the whole film; protect the afternoon for it) — 3.4 pp
- Day 3 (½) — bathroom inserts (one toothbrush / two), closet unpacking, pickups — 1.2 pp

## Casting note

Two men who read as seventeen years of friendship — the film lives in how they watch a game, not how they argue. The confrontation scene is the audition. Comic timing required; this is the funny-sad one on the slate.

## Assumptions & exclusions

- Assumes an apartment the company can access for three days at little or no cost — that assumption IS the project.
- Excludes reshoots, publicist, EPK. No tax-credit assumption.
