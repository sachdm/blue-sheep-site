# OPEN HOUSE — Log 01 · One Hundred Passes

**panel scrutiny · v1.0 → v1.1**

---

The line the panels policed hardest: Sam is never pathetic. He dresses one notch up, he never lies except "not yet," and the film declines to vote on whether the wall of feature sheets is sad — that ambiguity survived a hundred reads because it kept getting called the most honest thing in the script. Dara's "you're doing it wrong" moved from the midpoint to the first confrontation so the friendship gets screen time instead of the secret. Her confession mirrors his without matching it — eleven years to his one, professional camouflage to his amateur kind. The original ending sold Sam the maple house in a fantasy sequence; it was cut in pass 60 for the walk-up, because the point is aspiration scaled, not surrendered. "Takes a desk" does the work of a love scene.
