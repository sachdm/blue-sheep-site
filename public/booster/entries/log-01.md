The container is the concept: everything happens inside one battery charge. The ten-minute rule gives the film a clock without a villain, and passes 1-40 cut every beat that didn't fit inside it. June's widow reveal moved later three times — it has to land after the "walkie-talkie" joke, once the audience trusts her, not before. Her theology stays at one line ("Thank Gord") — longer drafts eulogized and died. Cole gives exactly one thing back, and it's the only thing he knows: cars at night. The life-support line carries both meanings or it carries nothing. Endings tested: a hug (no), a phone call shown (too much), a phone call heard over black with one word (yes). Both blinkers at an empty intersection stays — signalling to no one is the whole film in one image.
